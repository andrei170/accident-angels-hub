The Finding

A statewide referral service with zero local search footprint.

Your entire value is geographic: you connect a hurt person in a specific Ohio city to the right local doctor or attorney. But your site has no local SEO infrastructure to match. No Organization or Service schema, no per-city pages, no optimized title tags, no local citation strategy. When someone searches "car accident help Columbus" or "accident lawyer near me," there is almost nothing for Google to read and rank.

Picture the victim you exist to help

Someone gets rear-ended in Dayton, gets home shaken, and pulls out their phone to search "what to do after a car accident in Ohio" or "accident lawyer near me." That is your person, at the exact moment they need you. Right now a free 24/7 service built precisely for them is nearly invisible on that search, so they call the first billboard law firm or injury clinic instead. The traffic you should own is going somewhere else.

The fix is mostly plumbing, not a rebuildOrganization and Service schema, a set of programmatic per-city pages (Cincinnati, Columbus, Cleveland, Toledo, Akron, Dayton), and a local citation strategy. This is high-leverage groundwork, not a teardown of the site you have.
